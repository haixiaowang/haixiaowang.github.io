Welcome to my homepage! Starting from Sep 2026, I am a Pearson Fellow/Acting Instructor in the Department of Applied Mathematics at the University of Washington.

From Aug 2025 to Aug 2026, I was a Van Vleck visiting assistant professor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. Before that, I received my Ph.D. in mathematics from University of California San Diego, under the supervision of Professor Ioana Dumitriu. I received my B.S. in Physics and B.A. in Economics from Peking University.

My research focus on random matrix theory with its applications in statistics and mathematics of data science. Feel free to contact me if any of my research interests you.
